# Data Access and Licenses

This project evaluates Sleep-EDF as the source domain and SHHS/ISRUC as external domains.
Only code, aggregate metrics, and derived prediction summaries are intended for this repository;
raw PSG/EDF/REC files remain outside version control.

| Dataset | Official access route | Repository policy |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep-EDF | PhysioNet Sleep-EDF resource | Download separately and follow PhysioNet terms and citation requirements. |
| SHHS1/SHHS2 | NSRR SHHS resource | Download separately under the SHHS/NSRR data-use terms; do not redistribute raw PSG. |
| ISRUC Cohort I | ISRUC Sleep Database distribution | Request/access the cohort under its academic data-use conditions; do not redistribute raw PSG. |

Dataset terms can change. Before release or peer-review upload, verify the current portal terms,
required citations, and whether derived subject-level predictions may be shared. No claim is made
here that the datasets are open-license or suitable for unrestricted commercial use.
